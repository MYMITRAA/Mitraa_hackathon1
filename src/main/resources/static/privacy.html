Privacy Policy

This policy explains how MY MITRAA TECHNOLOGY PRIVATE LIMITED processes information for MiTRAA Hackathons 2026.

Information we collect

Account and contact details, date of birth, country and city, participation type, selected domain, consent records, team-member details, verification status/provider reference, payment order and transaction references, POC content, support communications and security logs.

Winner and prize verification

Identity documents are requested only from provisional first, second and third place winners where reasonably necessary to confirm identity, age, eligibility, prize beneficiary and applicable tax information. Indian winners may voluntarily use Aadhaar through the designated secure process or provide another accepted government-issued identity document. International winners may provide a passport or accepted national identity document. For a minor winner, necessary parent or legally authorized guardian information may also be collected.

Winner documents must be submitted only through the organizer's designated secure channel. Access is restricted to authorized personnel and service providers that require it for verification or lawful prize processing. Identity-document copies will not be displayed to judges, other participants or ordinary administrators, and will not be accepted through ordinary email or messaging applications. Documents will be deleted or irreversibly minimized after verification and the expiry of the applicable dispute, accounting and legal-retention period; the retained audit record will contain only the minimum verification result and necessary transaction references.

Age eligibility

Date of birth supplied during registration is used to apply the published 10–35 age rule. Participants under 18 require guardian consent. Additional documentary verification is limited to provisional winners as described above unless fraud prevention or applicable law reasonably requires otherwise.

Purpose and lawful use

We use data to assess eligibility, operate registrations and teams, process payments, accept and evaluate POCs, communicate deadlines/results, issue prizes and certificates, prevent fraud, secure the platform and comply with law. Marketing communication is optional and separately recorded.

Sharing

Information may be processed by contracted hosting, payment, verification and email providers, jury members under confidentiality obligations, and authorities where legally required. Identity documents are not shared with the jury. We do not sell participant personal data.

Retention and rights

We retain data only for event operations, legal/accounting obligations, dispute handling and certificate verification, after which it is deleted or minimized according to the approved retention schedule. Subject to applicable law, participants may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or withdrawal of optional consent.

Security and international transfers

We use access controls, encryption in transit, password hashing, signed webhooks, audit records and restricted administration. International processing will use appropriate contractual and legal safeguards.
