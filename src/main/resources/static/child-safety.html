Young innovators deserve stronger safeguards.

Participants aged 10–17 require guardian consent and age assurance before payment or final participation.

Guardian responsibilities

The guardian confirms the participant’s age, reviews the Terms and Privacy Policy, approves participation and communication, and remains available for material event or safety decisions.

Platform safeguards

  • Provider-hosted age assurance with minimized retained data.
  • No direct public display of a minor’s contact details.
  • Moderated event channels and reporting process.
  • Restricted jury/administrator access based on role.
  • No private organizer-to-minor meetings without approved safeguards.

Urgent reports

Contact info@mitratechgroup.com or +91 99383 30784. Where immediate danger exists, contact local emergency services.

Final minor-participation procedures must be approved by qualified legal/safeguarding professionals for every launch jurisdiction.
