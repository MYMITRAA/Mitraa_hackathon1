Official Event Rules

Build fairly. Prove responsibly.

Dates

  • Registration: 5 September–5 October 2026
  • POC submission deadline: 25 October 2026
  • Top 10 jury showcase: schedule communicated to finalists
  • Results: 1 November 2026

Teams

Individuals may enter alone. Team entries require a total of three to five people, including the leader. A person may participate in only one active entry unless the organizer approves otherwise.

Submission requirements

Provide a project name, problem statement, solution, architecture/technology stack, repository, demo, measurable impact and responsible-AI considerations. Work must be accessible to the jury by the deadline.

Evaluation framework

  • Innovation and clarity: 20%
  • Real-world impact: 20%
  • Technical implementation: 20%
  • Feasibility and scalability: 15%
  • Responsible AI, privacy and security: 15%
  • Demo and communication: 10%

Provisional winners and prize claim

First, second and third place rankings are provisional until the relevant individual winner or every applicable winning-team member completes identity and eligibility verification. Indian winners may voluntarily use Aadhaar through the designated secure process or submit another accepted government-issued identity document. International winners may submit a passport or accepted national identity document. Additional bank-beneficiary and tax information may be required solely to lawfully issue the prize. A parent or legally authorized guardian must act for a minor winner.

The organizer will communicate a reasonable verification deadline and secure submission method. Documents sent through ordinary email or messaging applications will not be accepted. If a provisional winner does not complete verification, supplies false information or is ineligible, the organizer may disqualify that entry and elevate the next eligible entry after providing a reasonable opportunity to resolve an apparent error.

Disqualification

Grounds include ineligibility, false information, plagiarism, harassment, malicious code, unlicensed assets, unauthorized data use, payment fraud, jury interference or deadline violations. Organizer and jury decisions are final subject to applicable law and published appeal procedures.

Rewards

First ₹50,000; second ₹20,000; third ₹5,000. Eligible participants receive participation certificates and may be considered for internship opportunities; neither certificate nor internship is guaranteed where rules are not completed.
