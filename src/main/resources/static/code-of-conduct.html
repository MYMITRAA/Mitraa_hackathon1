A safe arena for every builder.

Participants, guardians, organizers, mentors and jury members must communicate respectfully and professionally across all online and offline event spaces.

Expected behaviour

  • Respect identity, culture, ability, age, background and experience.
  • Protect confidential, personal and security-sensitive information.
  • Give credit for ideas, code, datasets and creative work.
  • Use AI responsibly and disclose material AI assistance where requested.
  • Report safety, harassment, cheating or security concerns promptly.

Prohibited behaviour

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content involving minors, stalking, doxxing, bullying, discrimination, sabotage, plagiarism, credential sharing and malicious technical activity are prohibited.

Reporting and response

Report concerns to info@mitratechgroup.com or +91 99383 30784. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Measures may include warning, content removal, access restriction, disqualification and referral to authorities.
