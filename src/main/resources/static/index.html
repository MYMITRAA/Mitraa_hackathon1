Mission Briefing

Know before launch.

How is age validated?

The platform uses provider-hosted age assurance. Participants under 18 require guardian consent. We retain verification status and provider reference—not raw identity documents.

How does international payment work?

The gateway order is created in USD. Registration becomes confirmed only after a signed payment webhook is verified by the backend.

What must a team submit?

A problem statement, solution summary, architecture, technology stack, repository, demo link, measurable impact and responsible-AI considerations.

Will everyone receive a certificate?

Eligible participants who meet published participation rules receive a digitally verifiable certificate; finalists and winners receive achievement certificates.
