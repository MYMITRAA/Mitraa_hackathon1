Refund Policy

Registration fees cover event administration and gateway processing. Requests must include the registration ID and payment reference.

Eligible refunds

Duplicate charges, verified technical payment errors, organizer cancellation, or a legally required refund may qualify. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within the gateway/bank processing period.

Generally non-refundable

Change of mind, failure to complete verification, missed deadlines, rule violations or disqualification are generally non-refundable unless applicable law requires otherwise.

How to request

Email info@mitratechgroup.com within seven days of payment and before the POC deadline. Include registration ID, payer name, transaction reference and reason. Do not email card or bank credentials.
