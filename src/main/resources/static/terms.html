Terms of Participation

Organizer and acceptance

MiTRAA Hackathons 2026 is organized by MY MITRAA TECHNOLOGY PRIVATE LIMITED. By registering, participants agree to these Terms, the Privacy Policy, Hackathon Rules and Code of Conduct.

Eligibility

Participants must be 10–35 years old on registration. Minors require verifiable guardian consent. Eligibility and payment must be completed before final participation.

Fees

India: ₹14.01 individual and ₹19.93 per team. Other countries: $2.70 individual and $5.80 per team. Team registration requires a total of three to five people, including the team leader. Taxes or gateway charges, if applicable and legally required, will be disclosed before payment.

Winner identity and prize verification

Participants provisionally ranked first, second or third must complete identity, age, payment-beneficiary and tax verification before a result or prize is finalized. An Indian winner may voluntarily provide Aadhaar through the organizer's designated secure verification process or provide another accepted government-issued identity document. A winner outside India may provide a passport or other accepted national identity document. A minor winner's parent or legally authorized guardian must complete the required verification and prize-claim documentation.

Verification documents must be submitted only through the secure channel specified by the organizer and never by ordinary email, messaging applications or public links. The organizer will collect only information reasonably necessary to validate eligibility and process the prize. Failure to complete verification by the communicated deadline, submission of false or inconsistent information, or an inability to establish eligibility may result in disqualification and selection of the next eligible entry, subject to applicable law and a reasonable opportunity to correct an error.

Participants retain ownership of original work. They grant the organizer a non-exclusive permission to review, demonstrate and promote submitted material for event administration and publicity, with appropriate attribution and subject to confidential-information rules.

Originality and acceptable use

Submissions must be lawful, original or properly licensed, secure, non-discriminatory and free from malicious code. Plagiarism, impersonation, payment fraud, harmful content and unauthorized personal-data use may result in disqualification.
